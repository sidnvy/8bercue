8bercue privacy information — 20 October 2026

Publisher: sid (individual)
Privacy and support: support@sidnvy.com

8bercue is independent of 5ber. It does not require a 5ber account and does not
send your activation details to a 5ber service.

The app reads your eUICC and installed profiles to show their status and verify
changes. EID and profile identifiers are masked on screen. Activation and
confirmation codes are used for the installation you confirm; they are not
included in diagnostics or transaction logs. Leaving the app clears the entered
activation draft. Avoid sharing activation codes or screenshots containing them.

When you confirm installation, the on-phone service communicates over HTTPS
with the operator address in your activation code. The operator receives the
activation credentials, protocol messages and card identity/certificates required
by the eSIM provisioning protocol, plus your network address. An installation
notification is sent to the recipient verified in that transaction. Operator
privacy policies apply to their processing. Local nickname and profile state
operations do not require an 8bercue account or an 8bercue server.

Camera access is optional and used to scan an activation QR code. Selected QR
images are decoded on your phone. There is no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.
The computer connection starts an authenticated ADB Shell service on your phone.
No separate privilege-manager app is required.

Transaction records contain hashes of card/profile identity, operation state,
timestamps and verification metadata. Notification records also contain the
operator host and delivery sequence. These records stay on your phone to prevent
unsafe retries. Privileged service records may remain after app uninstallation.
Unresolved records must not be removed as a way to retry an uncertain operation.
There is no automatic expiry for these safety records. They are retained locally
until deliberately removed after all pending operations have been resolved.
Uninstalling the app alone does not remove Shell-owned records. Contact support
for a reviewed removal procedure; do not send activation codes, full EIDs, or
private protocol payloads in a support request. The publisher cannot remotely
read or erase these local records.

8bercue has no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK and does not routinely
upload screen views, clicks, connection attempts or device identifiers.
A bounded, in-memory list of predefined connection stages and error codes
supports troubleshooting without automatic network telemetry.

Optional crash summaries are disabled by default. When you explicitly enable
crash reporting in Settings, a Sentry-compatible SDK sends limited failure
class and app code location to GlitchTip. Raw exception messages, full stack
traces, screenshots, card content, activation details, APDUs, and unrestricted
logs are excluded. You can disable future crash reporting in Settings.

After a failed ADB connection or verified card-read error, you may review a
one-time compatibility report with Android version/API level, manufacturer,
device model, ROM build ID, security patch level, app version, connection stage
and normalized failure code. Choosing Send diagnostic report transmits only
these allowlisted fields to GlitchTip. Canceling sends nothing, and one-time
submission does not enable future crash reporting. The provider may process
connection metadata, including the originating IP address; its own privacy
policy applies to that processing. A missing ADB handshake does not prove ROM
incompatibility. You can alternatively email support or save a diagnostic ZIP.

Diagnostics are exported only when you choose to share them. They exclude raw
identifiers, profile names, activation codes and protocol logs. Review the export
before choosing a recipient. App backup is disabled.

No card inventory or activation data is automatically sent to sid. If you email
support, your email address and anything you choose to include are received by
the publisher and the email service provider to handle your request. Ask the
privacy contact about access or deletion of support correspondence. Operator
account, provisioning and network records are controlled by the relevant operator.
