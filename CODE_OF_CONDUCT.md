# Community conduct

Help people with different phones, cards and experience levels participate.
These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and discussions.

- Be respectful. Explain technical disagreements using observable behavior and
  evidence; avoid personal attacks, harassment and discrimination.
- Welcome incomplete reports. Ask for the missing detail that helps reproduce
  an issue, and distinguish a confirmed finding from a hypothesis.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's identifiers, credentials,
  activation codes or private logs. Ask before sharing someone else's material.
- Keep testing voluntary. Do not pressure a contributor to risk a working
  subscription or disclose private data to prove compatibility.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and give others time to respond.

For a concern that should not be public, contact the maintainer at
[support@sidnvy.com](mailto:support@sidnvy.com). Include the relevant issue or PR
link and a concise description; omit unrelated personal information.

The maintainer may ask for edits, remove harmful content or restrict
participation to keep the project usable and welcoming.

For the technical contribution process, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
